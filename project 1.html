3. MERSAL- 49 million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION: ₹260 crore

Mersal is a 2017 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film[2] directed by Atlee and produced by Thenandal Studio Limited, in the studio's hundredth film production. The film stars Vijay in triple roles, alongside Nithya Menen, S. J. Suryah, Kajal Aggarwal, Samantha Ruth Prabhu, Vadivelu, Sathyaraj, Hareesh Peradi, Kovai Sarala and Sathyan and others. It is the second out of the three collaboration between Vijay and Atlee, before they began working with others. The film follows two brothers who were separated after the younger one's birth; Vetri, a vigilante magician who is against the medical crimes and Maaran, a reputed doctor who charges meagre amounts from his patients.
